              Connection troubleshooting
              
Press Scan; the connected EVE chip is detected automatically. FT4222 and MPSSE adapters are supported.

              
If the board is not detected, check that it is powered, the SPI connections are correct, and the FTDI adapter uses the FTDI D2XX/bus driver (ftdibus.inf, shown as USB Serial Converter). WinUSB or USB COM drivers, often installed with Zadig, are not compatible.

              
A board that does not answer at the selected SPI clock is retried at 12, 6 and 3 MHz automatically.

              
A Raspberry Pi Pico running eve_touch_bridge.uf2 is a full programmer: it owns the SPI bus (GP2 SCK, GP3 MOSI, GP4 MISO, GP5 CS#) and drives the live monitor and the settings pages. Flash the UF2 from pico/ in this extension once, then plug the Pico in — no BOOTSEL needed afterwards.
